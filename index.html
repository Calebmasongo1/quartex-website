          Net pay and PAYE

          Monthly gross to take-home, with every statutory deduction shown, and what the employee costs the employer.

          Includes: the five monthly PAYE bands, personal relief of KES 2,400, and NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy as allowable deductions before tax. Employer cost adds the employer's NSSF and Housing Levy. Excludes: pension contributions beyond NSSF, insurance and other reliefs, benefits in kind, allowances taxed differently, non-resident rules and the NITA levy. Rounding: nearest shilling, for resident employees on a monthly payroll. Sources: KRA, PAYE rates and reliefs; NSSF Year 4 limits from February 2026. Reviewed .

          VAT at 16%

          Add VAT to a net price, or work out how much VAT sits inside a gross amount.

          Includes: the standard rate of 16% on a taxable supply. Excludes: zero-rated and exempt supplies, withholding VAT, and input VAT you may claim. Worth knowing: registration is required once taxable supplies reach KES 5 million in 12 months; returns and payment are due by the 20th of the following month, nil returns included. Source: KRA, Value Added Tax. Reviewed .

          Turnover tax

          For resident businesses with annual sales above KES 1 million and up to KES 25 million. A final tax on gross monthly sales, with no expenses deducted.

          Includes: turnover tax at 1.5% of gross sales, filed and paid by the 20th of the following month. A late return carries a penalty of KES 1,000 a month. Excludes: rental income, management, professional and training fees, and income already taxed at source, which are not subject to turnover tax. Non-residents are excluded. Source: KRA, Turnover Tax. Reviewed .

          Monthly rental income tax

          For resident landlords of residential property earning between KES 280,000 and KES 15 million in rent a year.

          Includes: monthly rental income tax at 7.5% of gross rent, no expenses deducted, filed and paid by the 20th of the following month. Excludes: commercial rent, landlords outside the band and non-residents, who declare rent under the normal income tax rules. Source: KRA, Rental Income Tax. Reviewed .

          What a missed return is likely to cost, so you can decide today whether to file now and pay later, or clear it in one go.

          Includes: the late-filing penalty for the return selected; where the tax is also unpaid, a late-payment penalty of 5% of the tax due and interest of 1% for each month or part of a month.             The firm

            Connected challenges. Considered answers.

            Quartex Synergy & Associates is a multidisciplinary professional services and business advisory firm in Westlands, Nairobi, serving corporates, SMEs, NGOs, SACCOs, government institutions, development partners and entrepreneurs.

            We keep audit, tax, accounting, risk, compliance and advisory under one roof because clients' questions overlap: financial records affect management decisions, tax obligations influence cash flow, internal controls affect operational reliability, and governance shapes accountability. By bringing the disciplines together we address issues in context rather than as isolated tasks.

            Each discipline has a named lead. Every engagement starts with a written scope and fee, is carried out and documented to professional standards, and ends with findings explained in plain language and followed up until they are in place.

            Our work is guided by integrity, precision, innovation, independence and confidentiality, and informed by relevant professional frameworks and Kenyan regulatory requirements.
